## Image/video fidelity and cropping

- **All 37 separately uploaded portfolio photographs are preserved byte-for-byte** in `assets/media/original-photos/`. Earlier original-media assets (Muse, FRC, Splittr, DVD Pong) remain at their original paths.

- Display photographs were resized **without cropping** to a maximum of 1900×1900 pixels and converted to WebP (quality 84); aspect ratios are preserved. Main project media, galleries and video posters are displayed with `object-fit: contain`, which avoids cut-off robot parts. No people were digitally removed.

- **The one intentionally cropped derivative is the ASCII portrait** from `D7F83E86-C713-477F-9046-4B16635B069F_1_105_c.jpeg`: its original dimensions are 768×1024 pixels and the face/shoulder study samples rectangle **x=130–660, y=35–645**, about 69% of the width and 60% of the height. This crop is applied only to the character-rendered portrait; the original photograph and its separate full-frame image are untouched.

- Video posters are resized still frames; no subject was removed. Website MP4s are optimized for web playback (up to 960px wide, 24 fps, H.264, sound removed). Full original camera recordings were not re-encoded in-place or deleted. For frame-accurate research, use the user-provided original videos.

- On restricted aspect ratios, a viewer may show blank margins. That is intentional: it preserves source content instead of hiding the action behind a crop.
